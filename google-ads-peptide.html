The Real Problem

Your Ads Don't Get Beaten. They Get Shut Down.

Every peptide founder has the same story. The account runs for a few weeks. Then a disapproval. Then a suspension. Then an appeal that goes nowhere, and a revenue line that falls off a cliff overnight.

It is almost never the product. It is how the account was built.

Failure 01

The structure invites the review

Most agencies build a peptide account exactly like any other ecommerce account. Google's health and supplement policies do not treat it like any other account. The structure itself is what pulls the first manual review, before a single ad is judged on its wording.

Failure 02

The ad copy reads like a claim

Peptide advertising lives and dies on wording. One implied outcome, one dosage reference, one word borrowed from your Instagram captions, and the ad is disapproved. Enough disapprovals and the flag moves from the ad to the account.

Failure 03

The landing page fails review

Your ad can be spotless and still get killed by the page behind it. Claims above the fold, missing research-use language, the wrong checkout flow. Reviewers read the destination, and most agencies never touch it.

We have solved all three, on real peptide accounts, repeatedly. That is the entire job.
